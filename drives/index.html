Optimal Drives

What’s the shortest drive that hits every Chick-fil-A? Every NHL arena? Every town named Salem? I run a routing engine on my own computer, solve the route, and animate it as a one-minute video.

Six of them

There are more than 30 so far. Most ideas now come from viewer comments.

How they’re made

Each video starts with a count: what qualifies as a stop and what gets left out. Locations come from store locators, OpenStreetMap, and the Census geocoder, cross-checked against each other. A self-hosted OSRM server builds the drive-time matrix between every pair of stops, and the LKH solver finds the visiting order. Hawaii and Puerto Rico go by plane. Alaska goes by the Alaska Highway.

The animation is Python drawing frame by frame, encoded with ffmpeg. The 3D ones use three.js rendered through headless Chrome. The drive-time maps (airports, ERs, nearest coast) skip the tour and color every road by its travel time to the closest destination.

The routing side also shows up in Optitrek, a road-trip optimizer for national parks.
