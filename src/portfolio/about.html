About

I’m Max. I live in Salem, Massachusetts, and I make maps.

I finished an M.S. in Geo-Information Science at Salem State in May 2025. Since then I’ve been building the things on this site: a statewide atlas of every public school in Massachusetts, lidar studies of wooded land, a live transportation map of New England, and a study of the four towns flooded to make the Quabbin Reservoir.

This fall it’s been two things. I’m doing the 30 Day Map Challenge, a print map a day through November. And I run a routing engine on my own computer to make Optimal Drives, short videos that answer questions like how long it takes to drive to every NHL arena.

During the day I teach probability, statistics, and financial algebra at Lynn English High School. This is my fourth year. I write my own curriculum, and I built lehsmath.com for the math department. Before teaching I spent eight years running hotels in New Hampshire and Boston.

Most of my work happens in QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, R, Python, PostGIS, and MapLibre, with Illustrator for anything that gets printed.

Games, fantasy maps, and browser toys live on a separate site, Mapzimus.

I’m looking for GIS analyst and web GIS developer work, in person or hybrid in New England, or remote. My résumé is here.
